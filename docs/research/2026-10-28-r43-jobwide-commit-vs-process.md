## Grounding
Official Microsoft JOBOBJECT_EXTENDED_LIMIT_INFORMATION documentation distinguishes ProcessMemoryLimit with JOB_OBJECT_LIMIT_PROCESS_MEMORY and JobMemoryLimit with JOB_OBJECT_LIMIT_JOB_MEMORY. These are INDEPENDENT enforceable committed virtual memory constraints; a process-specific cap can be larger than its job aggregate cap. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/winnt/ns-winnt-jobobject_extended_limit_information
